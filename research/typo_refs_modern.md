# Typography references: modern Europe, 2000–2026

These are facts for the kinetic type in "I'm Upping My P(doom) — EU Edition". The research was done on 1 Oct 2026. Every item gives a date, the text to quote, 1–3 hard numbers, a status and the source URLs.

**Status labels**
- **verified:** I opened a primary text (EUR-Lex/OJ PDF, Commission press release or speech PDF, company newsroom, statute) and the words or numbers are copied from it.
- **reported:** the fact comes from reputable press or legal sources, but I did not open the primary text, or it is a live figure that changes.
- **unverified:** a single source, a tracker, or something I could not confirm.

**Language note:** EU acts and EU speeches are quoted in their English version. That version is equally authentic with the other 23 language versions, so no separate "original" is given for them. German and Italian sources are quoted in the original first, then in English.

### 1.2 AI Act Art. 51(2): the 10^25 threshold
- **Quote (EN):** "A general-purpose AI model shall be presumed to have high impact capabilities … when the cumulative amount of computation used for its training measured in floating point operations is greater than 10^25."
- **Numbers:**
  - **10^25 FLOP**, i.e. 10,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000.
  - Art. 51(3) lets the Commission change the threshold by delegated act.
- **Draghi's gloss (report Part A, 9 Sep 2024):** "a threshold which some state-of-the-art models already exceed."
- **Status:** verified.
- **Sources:**
  - OJ PDF (above)
  - https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/51/

### 1.3 AI Act Art. 99: fines
- **Quote (EN), Art. 99(3):** "administrative fines of up to EUR 35 000 000 or, if the offender is an undertaking, up to 7 % of its total worldwide annual turnover … whichever is higher."
- **Numbers:**
  - **€35M / 7%** for prohibited practices (Art. 5).
  - **€15M / 3%** for other obligations, including Art. 50 transparency.
  - **€7.5M / 1%** for misleading information given to authorities.
  - For SMEs, the lower of the two amounts applies (Art. 99(6)).
- **Status:** verified.
- **Source:** https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/99/

### 2.8 EU Inc: the 28th regime (checking the claim in the brief)
- **Date:** **18 Mar 2026**. The proposal is **COM(2026) 321 final**, procedure 2026/0074(COD). It comes with a Communication on the wider 28th regime.
- **Quote (EN, Commission):** "founding an EU Inc. company within 48 hours, for less than €100 and with no minimum share capital requirements and by only submitting their company information once, via an EU-level interface".
- **Numbers:**
  - **48 h**, **< €100**, **€0** minimum capital.
  - It replaces "27 national legal systems and more than **60** company legal forms".
  - Target: agreement "by the end of 2026". Under Art. 109 of the proposal, it applies 12 months after entry into force.
- **Status:**
  - **Verified**: the claim in the brief ("EU Inc, 18 March 2026: a company within 48 hours for under €100") is correct.
  - It is still only **proposed, not adopted**. In Parliament, rapporteur René Repasi's JURI draft report drew **1,418** amendments by 17 Jul 2026, and no committee vote has been scheduled. In the Council, a second presidency compromise came out on about 30 Sep 2026, and trilogues are targeted for Nov 2026. These progress details are unverified (from the the28thregime.eu tracker).
  - The SOTU 2026 did **not** mention EU Inc. The SOTU 2025 did: "we are preparing the so-called 28th regime" (verified).
