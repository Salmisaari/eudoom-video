This is the AI and tech-culture layer on top of `addhd/eurodoom/research.md` (cited as RM#), plus the evergreen Europe-tech memes. The audience is SF people who dunk on Europe and Europeans who dunk on themselves.

**Keys:** [V] = checked against the cited page or post this session. [P] = partial (secondary source, search snippet, or blocked page). [U] = unverified. ★★★★★ = instantly recognised on tech Twitter; ★ = niche. L/V = likes/views on X, fetched 29 Sep.

**Method:** X was read through the fxtwitter embed API (exact text, dates and counts): 49 account timelines swept from 1 Aug to 29 Sep 2026 (13,334 posts), plus specific status IDs; xcancel returned HTTP 451. I also read the Hacker News front page for every day from 1 Aug to 28 Sep.
- The session's web-search allowance ran out early, so later checks are direct fetches. Reuters, Axios, CNBC, BBC and Guardian pages were blocked; anything resting only on them is [P].

### 1B. AI Act status card, Sept 2026 [V]
- **The law:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, in force since 1 Aug 2024. The Official Journal version is dated 12.7.2024 and runs 144 pages: 180 recitals, 113 articles, 13 annexes.
- **2 Feb 2025:** bans and AI literacy.
- **2 Aug 2025:** general-purpose AI (GPAI) obligations. Models already on the market have until 2 Aug 2027.
- **2 Aug 2026:** "the remainder of the AI Act starts to apply, unless specified otherwise".
  - The Art. 50 transparency rules (chatbot disclosure, deepfake labels) now apply. Legacy generative systems have until 2 Dec 2026 for the Art. 50(2) marking.
  - The Commission can fine GPAI providers from this date (Art. 101) [P, my reading].
  - Reactions: "As of today, the use of AI becomes more transparent in the EU" (@EU_Commission). "Most of the AI act's rules come into force today" (@EUCouncil, 43k V). A reply: "If I do social scoring in excel is that allowed?" (@petergostev).
- **2 Dec 2026:** new bans on AI that generates non-consensual intimate imagery or child sexual abuse material.
- **Delayed by the AI Omnibus** (in force 27 Jul 2026): high-risk obligations under Annex III move to 2 Dec 2027, and Annex I to 2 Aug 2028 (RM7).
- **Code of Practice** (10 Jul 2025): 21 signatories, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Mistral, Aleph Alpha and Black Forest Labs. xAI signed only the safety chapter; Meta refused.
- **"Stop the Clock"** (3 Jul 2025): 46 CEOs asked for a two-year "clock-stop", including Airbus, ASML, Mistral, Mercedes, Lufthansa, ElevenLabs and Black Forest Labs, plus Ravensburger (jigsaws) and Südzucker (sugar). The Commission: "There is no stopping the clock. There is no grace period. There is no pause." A year later the Omnibus paused it.
- **Enforcement, Aug–Sep 2026:** none listed; no fines found [U].
- **Src:** https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/ · https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/contents-code-gpai · http://web.archive.org/web/20250703161720/https://aichampions.eu/ · https://x.com/EU_Commission/status/2083818025858310439

The OJ page: a small EU flag at top left, "Official Journal of the European Union", "2024/1689", "12.7.2024", footer "1/144". A countdown: 2.2.25 → 2.8.25 → 2.8.26 → 2.12.26 → 2.12.27 → 2.8.28.
